Privacy Notice

Sample notice for testing. Dummy Bank is not a real bank.

What we collect

Name, mobile number, email, city, preferred language, the product you are interested in and your income band. We also record which campaign brought you to us (UTM parameters).

Why we collect it

  • Account opening – to create and service your account.
  • Sales follow-up – so a relationship manager can contact you about the product you asked for.
  • Marketing – offers on other products, only with your consent.
  • Analytics – cookies and trackers that help us improve the site, only with your consent.

Who we share it with

Your lead details are stored in our CRM (HubSpot). Your consent choices are recorded in ConsentIQ, our consent manager, and synced to the CRM so our teams only contact you for purposes you agreed to.

Your choices

You can review, change or withdraw consent at any time.
